# Discover the real intent

Before a plan exists, distinguish the requested artifact from the outcome: who needs it, what failure hurts, what success means, and which constraint binds. Offer your best current hypothesis; attach uncertainty to concrete missing information rather than a theatrical confidence score.

Watch for “I should probably,” generic scalability claims, borrowed best practices and unnamed “users.” Ask for a specific recent situation or what they would choose without needing to justify the convention. Keep recommendations revisable so polite agreement does not masquerade as evidence.

In frontier rounds, independent goals/constraints can be asked together. Use one-question mode when explicitly requested or when only one prerequisite question is ready. Do not force a numeric 95% confidence target. Stop when the outcome, success condition, binding constraints and relevant tradeoffs are shared and the user confirms the understanding.

Do not run an unattended interview in CI/autonomous contexts. Record genuinely blocking unknown choices for the human; inspect facts and continue independent work rather than guessing their preference.
