# Product Discovery

Start from a real problem and existing context. Do not apply founder or market-fit rituals to routine maintenance.

1. Establish who experiences the problem, their current workaround, frequency/consequence, why now and what success would look like.
2. Generate meaningfully different directions using selected [ideation and evaluation lenses](references/discovery-lenses.md). Converge after user reaction; do not present cosmetic variants as distinct strategies.
3. Choose the relevant mode: idea shaping, product viability, user journey friction or feature prioritization. Separate observed adoption/retention evidence from guesses; a billing library or README does not prove revenue or product-market fit.
4. Produce a concise brief with problem, direction, key assumptions, smallest learning experiment, explicit anti-goals, risks and next action. Save requested durable output under `docs/specs/<topic>-discovery.md`.
5. Handoff a chosen direction to [requirements](../requirements/SKILL.md). Use [prototyping](../prototyping/SKILL.md) for questions requiring a runnable artifact. Do not silently begin production implementation.
