# Brandkit

This is the explicitly selected visual direction for this surface. Apply its aesthetic rules within that scope; do not combine contradictory alternative styles. Existing brand requirements, truthful content, accessibility and reduced-motion behavior take precedence. Verify fonts, icons, dependencies and assets actually exist; source examples do not authorize installing packages or inventing evidence.

Create a cohesive identity board image, not unrelated logo variants or a functional design system export. Read [brand board specification](references/brand-board-specification.md) for the 3×3/2×3 panel systems, logo construction methods, visual modes and prompt template.

Identify brand name, audience, meaning, constraints and available approved assets. Build a logo concept from monogram/meaning, product action, metaphor fusion, negative space or construction geometry. Choose one concept and palette before arranging applications.

Generate a legible board showing identity, palette, typography and representative digital/physical applications. Keep board text sparse and coherent; use available image tooling and report its actual output. A raster mockup does not deliver editable vector logos, font licenses, trademark clearance or usable product screens; state the deliverable accurately.

Keep reference logos as inspiration unless authorized for reproduction. Do not invent factual claims about the business or present speculative applications as shipped work.
