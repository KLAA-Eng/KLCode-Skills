# Industrial Brutalist Ui

This is the explicitly selected visual direction for this surface. Apply its aesthetic rules within that scope; do not combine contradictory alternative styles. Existing brand requirements, truthful content, accessibility and reduced-motion behavior take precedence. Verify fonts, icons, dependencies and assets actually exist; source examples do not authorize installing packages or inventing evidence.

Choose **Swiss industrial print** (light newsprint, heavy grotesk type, visible grids) or **tactical telemetry** (dark mono data, restrained CRT cues); do not mix them arbitrarily within one interface. Read [industrial specification](references/industrial-specification.md) for palette, grid, semantics and texture recipes.

Use square geometry and precise compartmentalization with intentional contrast between dense readouts and large structural type. This mode permits Inter/heavy grotesks that other style alternatives discourage; its typography preference is scoped here.

Decorative crosshairs, barcodes, scanlines and revision labels must not masquerade as live engineering data or real legal claims. Label sample values and use actual telemetry for functional readouts. Texture must not obscure small text, focus indicators or high-contrast accessibility. Keep scanline/noise layers noninteractive and disable disruptive movement for reduced-motion users.
