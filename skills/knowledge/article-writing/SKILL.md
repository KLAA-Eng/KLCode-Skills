# Article Writing

Establish audience, purpose and medium; build an outline with one job per section. Lead with a concrete example/artifact/observation and explain what it establishes.

If a voice is requested, derive a short reusable profile from examples; otherwise use direct useful prose. Preserve the user's nuance rather than enforcing one founder/marketing persona. Do not invent experience, customer proof, statistics or credibility.

For technical guides show usable commands/code/output and the expected result. For essays keep one evidence-backed argument thread. For newsletters make the first screen useful. Remove generic throat-clearing, unsupported superlatives, padding and repeated conclusions.

Verify claims against supplied/retrieved sources and match medium formatting. Save only when requested, under docs/knowledge/ by default. Drafting does not authorize publishing or distribution. Research follows technical-research or literature-review when evidence is needed.
