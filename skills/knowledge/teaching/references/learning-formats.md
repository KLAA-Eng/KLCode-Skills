# Local learning workspace

Default base: docs/knowledge/learning/<topic>/; paths are relative to that workspace.
- mission.md: practical why, observable success, prior knowledge/constraints and explicit exclusions.
- resources.md: high-trust sources with what each teaches and why relevant.
- lessons/NNNN-<topic>.html: one short tangible learning outcome, explanation and exercise/feedback.
- reference/: compressed syntax/algorithms/glossary/cheat sheets worth revisiting.
- learning-records/NNNN-<insight>.md: demonstrated understanding, corrected misconception, stated prior knowledge with depth, or a mission shift; evidence and implication where useful.
- assets/: reusable local stylesheet/widgets when more than one lesson needs them.
- notes.md: stated teaching preferences.

Create lazily. One mission per focused workspace; avoid teaching abstract material unrelated to it. A record says what is now understood and why that changes the next lesson, not merely what was shown. Supersede corrected records rather than silently deleting the learning history.

Use recall, spaced practice and interleaving for durable skills; keep new explanation simple enough for working memory. Vary answer placement without artificial equal-character constraints. Local HTML should remain accessible and useful without a hosted model/provider.
