# Teaching

For an ongoing learning request, use docs/knowledge/learning/<topic>/ by default. Read [the teaching workspace formats](references/learning-formats.md). Clarify the concrete mission, prior ability and constraints; do not create a course workspace for a simple factual explanation.

Teach one tangible win at a time, within the learner's next reachable challenge. Ground knowledge in trusted resources and cite the relevant source. Combine explanation with effortful recall, worked exercises and immediate feedback. Space/interleave practice for retention rather than mistaking familiarity for mastery.

Short HTML lessons and compact references may share local assets; accessible readable output matters more than elaborate widgets. Record demonstrated understanding, corrected misconceptions and relevant prior knowledge, not merely topics covered. Update the mission when the learner's actual goal changes.

Offer authentic practice or a relevant community when useful, respecting a preference not to join. Do not prescribe impossible equal-character answer options or force every lesson into the same widget.
