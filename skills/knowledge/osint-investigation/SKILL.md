# Osint Investigation

Use lawful public sources and authorized supplied artifacts for a relevant claim. This separately licensed adaptation is CC-BY-SA-4.0; read [attribution](SOURCES.md).

Define the exact claim, relevant date/precision, permitted scope and stop condition. Separate nearby claims such as shared hosting versus ownership, upload versus capture time, or visual similarity versus an exact place/object match.

Read [case framing and evidence records](references/case-framing.md), then [decisive tests and convergence](references/testing-and-convergence.md). Inspect originals, track source lineage, test the cheapest meaningful falsifier and retain unresolved contradictions. A blocked fetch is an evidence gap, not disproof.

Use [artifact and reporting discipline](references/artifacts-and-reporting.md) for images, metadata, transformations and provisional conclusions. Do not identify private people by face, locate homes, infer sensitive traits, collect credentials/breach dumps or bypass controls. Keep infrastructure research passive and threat work defensive.

No provider/toolkit is mandatory. Do not claim an unperformed check. Save requested case notes under docs/knowledge/; no automatic uploads, outreach, publication or monitoring. Cite sources and distinguish findings from hypotheses.
