# Review protocol

Question: domain/system, intervention/method, comparison and outcome; PICO where appropriate. Choose scope, dates, languages, publication types, databases/registries and inclusion/exclusion before screening. Keep changes to the protocol visible.

Log source, actual search date, exact query/filters, retrieved count, export and coverage limits. Choose databases suited to the domain; optional broad web discovery supplements rather than silently replacing systematic retrieval. Do not claim example dates/counts were executed.

Deduplicate DOI, recognized IDs and normalized bibliographic identity. Then link reports of the same underlying study: preprint/journal article, protocol/follow-up and overlapping cohorts must not count as independent evidence.

Screen title/abstract/full text and retain exclusion reasons for systematic work. Unavailable full text is an explicit limitation. Extraction includes study/report ID, design, sample/data, method, comparator/baseline, metric/outcome, result, limitations and reproducibility.

Synthesize themes, conflicts and strength of evidence, not a paragraph-per-paper catalog. Distinguish replicated robust findings from limited/single-source claims. Publication bias, missing/negative findings and dataset/population limits matter.

Verify bibliographic identifiers, authors/year/version, preprint status and source-to-claim support. DOI registration/metadata checks establish identity, not existence of a supported scientific claim. Keep raw IDs/exports/notes separate from final prose. Optional citation styles/database strategy resources provide mode detail.
