# Literature Review

Choose narrative/scoping/systematic depth to fit the request; publication/high-consequence claims need stronger search and appraisal than orientation. Define the question, domain, comparison and outcome; use PICO where relevant.

Use [the review protocol](references/review-protocol.md). Preserve search strings/dates/filters/source coverage, deduplicate records and link multiple reports of one study. Record screening/exclusion and unavailable full texts honestly. Extract methods, data, baselines, outcomes and limitations before thematic synthesis.

Separate strong/limited/speculative conclusions and label preprints/reviews. Verify identifiers and that cited sources actually support the claim; a registered DOI does not verify the paper's methods or relevance.

The imported [local literature tools](references/local-tools.md) process normalized records and optionally check DOI metadata. Online requests, paid services, PDF export packages and model-based diagrams are not required. Save durable review/protocol/extraction notes under docs/knowledge/<topic>/. Do not claim an illustrative command/search result was executed.
