# Decision record

# NNNN: <Decision>
Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Deprecated
Date: <actual decision or reconstruction date>

## Context
Problem, constraints, actual evidence and why a decision was needed.

## Decision
Selected approach and decisive rationale; name the owner/confirmation when available.

## Alternatives
Real considered alternatives, benefits/costs and why rejected. Do not invent a competition to justify a foregone choice.

## Consequences
Benefits, tradeoffs, risks, follow-up conditions and reversal cost.

## Links
Relevant specification/plan, evidence and predecessor/successor decisions.

Preserve prior records and sequence. When superseding, link both records and amend status rather than rewriting old rationale into a history that never happened. A reconstructed record labels observed history versus inferred reasons and remains proposed until confirmed.
