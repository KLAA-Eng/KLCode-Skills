# Scholarly Evaluation

Identify the work and requested review scope; do not turn work-level feedback into person ranking or high-impact personnel/funding/publication decisions. Read [responsible assessment](references/responsible_assessment.md) for organizational use.

For ordinary developmental feedback, use [the evidence rubric](references/work-rubric.md): research question, context, methods, data, analysis, results, limitations, writing and citations; N/A where inapplicable. Tie each criticism to evidence and a concrete revision, separating missing information from disproven claims.

For an explicitly selected structured assessment/calibration exercise, read [local tooling](references/local_tooling.md) and [evaluation framework](references/evaluation_framework.md). Bundled scripts use bounded local JSON/CSV and no external packages/network. Structural validity and weighted scores do not establish psychometric validity or truth.

Preserve contrary evidence, uncertainty, rater disagreement and sensitivity. Save requested reports under docs/knowledge/. No model judge, paid API or automatic acceptance/rejection verdict is required.
