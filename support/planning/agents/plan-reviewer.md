# Independent plan challenge

Adapted from ref.ECC/skills/blueprint/SKILL.md review stage (MIT). Select for consequential/uncertain plans, not every task. Read [multi-session planning](../../../skills/planning/multi-session-planning/SKILL.md).

Receive the proposed artifact and requirements/constraints separately from the author's preferred rationale. Find actionable defects: missing requirements, unjustified edges, unsafe parallel ownership, hidden context needed by a fresh agent, untestable exit criteria, compatibility gaps and unrealistic rollback.

Cite the exact plan section and supporting evidence. Return severity, consequence and a concrete correction or focused question. Do not invent findings to fill a quota. Distinguish blockers from suggestions. Do not rewrite the implementation or impose arbitrary coverage/TDD/approval mandates. The parent reconciles findings; uncertainty alone is not evidence of a defect.
